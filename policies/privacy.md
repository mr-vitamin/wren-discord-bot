# Privacy Policy

**Last revised:** October 7, 2026  
**Operator:** Independent developer of Wren  
**Privacy contact:** contact.wrenbot@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the independent developer operating Wren (the **“Operator,” “we,” “us,”** or **“our”**) handles information through the application. The Operator can be contacted at the privacy address above. Discord separately processes information under its own privacy policy; this document does not govern Discord’s systems or the decisions of a server’s administrators.

## Scope and sources of information

Wren receives information when a person uses an available function and, where needed, from Discord’s API to carry out that request. It is not configured to read ordinary server messages or direct messages. Wren does not ask for a person’s legal name, address, payment details, or file uploads. A user may nevertheless include personal information in material submitted through the Service. Users should not submit sensitive personal information unless necessary and appropriate.

## Categories of information processed

Depending on the function used, Wren may process information a user submits, including text, titles, links, and selected attributions; Discord user IDs and available account or server display names; server and channel identifiers; information entered by authorized server staff, such as a record’s status or assigned person; and creation or update times. A locally stored record may also receive an identifier generated by Wren. Wren does not maintain a separate local copy of every item it posts to Discord and does not download or store files linked by users.

Wren also receives interaction metadata and server context needed to respond through Discord. Local operational logs may include errors and details of a failed interaction. The application’s access token is stored separately from user records; it is not user-submitted content.

## Purposes of processing

We use this information to respond to requests, provide and maintain the Service, keep records created through authorized functions, display requested information to people with access to the relevant Discord channel, troubleshoot failures, and address misuse or security incidents. We do not use this information to build advertising profiles, sell data, or train an AI model.

## Visibility and disclosure

Information Wren posts to Discord is visible to people with access to the destination channel. Records returned in a Discord channel are visible to people with access to that channel. The Operator can access locally stored records and logs. Discord server administrators control membership and channel permissions; Wren does not provide per-record privacy controls within a channel.

Wren uses Discord’s API to receive interactions and post messages. Discord may process the information under its own privacy policy. The Operator does not send locally stored user records to a separate analytics or advertising provider and does not sell or license them to data brokers. Information may be disclosed where required by applicable law, subject to applicable protections.

## Storage and security

Certain records are stored locally by the Operator. **Those records are not currently encrypted at rest.** There is no automated retention or deletion mechanism for them. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Retention and deletion

Locally stored records remain until the Operator removes them because they are no longer needed or in response to a valid request. At present, there is no automatic deletion period or user-facing deletion command. Local error logs may remain until the Operator removes them. If Wren is discontinued, locally held Discord API data will be deleted unless retention is required by law.

Information posted to Discord remains subject to the server’s moderation choices and Discord’s retention practices. Removing a local record does not automatically delete a Discord message; deleting a Discord message does not automatically remove a local record.

## Requests to access, correct, or delete information

To ask what Wren holds about you or request correction or deletion, contact **contact.wrenbot@gmail.com**. Include a relevant record identifier or message link if available, and avoid sending unnecessary personal information. We may ask for reasonable confirmation that you are the affected person or are authorized to act for them. We will review requests without unnecessary delay and explain any legal reason that prevents us from fulfilling one.

If your request concerns information held by Discord, you may also need to contact the server’s moderators or Discord. Wren cannot edit Discord’s independent records or override a server administrator’s channel permissions.

## Children and age requirements

Wren is intended for people who are permitted to use Discord under its applicable age rules. It is not designed to knowingly collect information from children who are prohibited from using Discord. If you believe Wren holds such information, use the privacy contact above so the Operator can review and remove it where appropriate. A server may have additional age rules that its administrators, not Wren, enforce.

## International use and legal rights

People may use Discord from different locations. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights concerning access, correction, deletion, objection, or other handling of your personal information. Requests can be made through the privacy contact above. This policy does not waive rights provided by applicable law.

## Changes to this policy

We will update this policy if a change to Wren materially changes the categories of information handled, purposes of processing, recipients, storage, or retention practices. A new function alone does not necessarily require a new policy. We will revise the date above and provide notice of material changes through an appropriate channel or another reasonable means where practicable.

## Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact **contact.wrenbot@gmail.com**.
